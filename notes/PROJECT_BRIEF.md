# Hinglish-Assistant Project Brief

## What this project is
Fine-tuned Qwen2.5-3B with QLoRA to match GPT-4o-mini on Hinglish (code-mixed Hindi-English) 
conversational quality at ~30× lower serving cost. Two halves:
1. Fine-tuning (training side) — DONE
2. vLLM serving + benchmarks — IN PROGRESS

## Current state (end of Week 3)

### Training (Week 3 Day 2 complete)
- Base: Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, 4-bit NF4
- Method: QLoRA rank=16, alpha=32, all linear layers (~30M trainable params)
- Hyperparams: lr=2e-4, batch_size=16 effective, 2 epochs, bf16, adamw_8bit
- Training: 9.2 min on Blackwell GPU, final loss 0.95
- Adapter saved to: /content/drive/MyDrive/hinglish-assistant/models/qlora-r16-2ep-final
- Adapter size: 125 MB
- W&B run: 3yenkm1g

### Dataset (Week 2 complete)
- 10,594 clean synthetic Hinglish instruction examples
- 4 categories: casual (3391), customer_support (2880), question (2847), sentiment (1476)
- 100% Roman script
- Generated by GPT-4o-mini, methodology in notes/week2_dataset_decisions.md

## Defensible headline claim
"Fine-tuned Qwen-3B achieves register quality matching/exceeding GPT-4o-mini at <1/30th 
serving cost. Trades ~8% overall quality for dramatic cost savings and superior Hinglish 
naturalness."

## Known limitations (always acknowledge in writeup)
1. Roman script only — mixed-script (Devanagari) deferred to v2
2. Conversational > instructional — sometimes confuses sentiment vs intent
3. Synthetic training data from GPT-4o-mini — mild stylistic inheritance
4. N=50 eval set is small

## Methodology choices
- GPT-4o-mini for data generation: chose after evaluating Gemini Flash, Llama 3.1 8B, 
  Llama 3.3 70B. Decision in notes/week2_dataset_decisions.md
- Claude Sonnet as judge: chose to break circularity (training data came from GPT-4o-mini)
- QLoRA over full fine-tuning: deployment constraint (model serves at 4-bit on T4)
